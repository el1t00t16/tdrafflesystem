## 🌟 Welcome & Purpose

Dear Teachers' Committee Members, School Heads, and Supervisors,

On Teachers' Day 2026, over **2,000 teachers and school personnel** will gather at the Malungon Gymnasium. In past years, drawing raffle prizes by hand using paper stubs in a *tambiolo* took **3 to 5 exhausting hours**, caused paper tickets to get lost or stuck, and left teachers wondering if everyone truly had a fair chance.

This digital raffle system was created specifically to solve those problems. It is designed to be:
- **100% Honest & Fair:** No favoritism, no secret buttons, no repeat winners.
- **Exciting on the Big Screen:** The whole gymnasium can watch their district spin, hear the countdown, and celebrate the winners with confetti!
- **Fast & Smooth:** Gate registration takes seconds, minor prizes are drawn in batches, and teachers claim their prizes with zero confusion.

## 🛡️ The 3 Golden Guarantees for Our Teachers

### 1. Absolute Fairness (No "Palakasan")
Every eligible teacher has **exactly one equal chance** in the system. The computer mixes names randomly (using the same certified mathematical shuffling algorithm used by national lotteries). No one—not even the computer technician—can predict or pre-select who will win.

### 2. Equal Stage Visibility (The 5 Districts)
On the gymnasium's giant LED wall, all **5 educational groupings** are displayed side-by-side:
1. **North District**
2. **East District**
3. **West District**
4. **South District**
5. **Private Schools + Daycare (ECCD) + Local School Board (LSB)**

Teachers from remote mountain schools and private institutions have the exact same visibility and odds as teachers from central schools.

### 3. No Mistakes on Stage (The "Check First" Rule)
When the wheel spins on screen, the names shown are **provisional (temporary)**. The committee chair or emcee reviews the name first:
- If the teacher is qualified, click **"Confirm Winners"** $\rightarrow$ it becomes official!
- If the teacher is absent (if the committee rules require physical presence) or disqualified, click **"Redraw"** $\rightarrow$ the system picks a fresh name with **zero damage to records or prize counts**.

| Personnel Group | Eligible for Raffle? | Details & Rules |

| **Classroom Teachers** (Teacher I, II, III, Master Teachers, Head Teachers) | ✅ **ELIGIBLE (ALL RAFFLES)** | Qualified for both **Minor Prizes** and the **Grand Prize** upon gate check-in / active registration. |

| **Non-Teaching Personnel** (Utility, Clerks, Security, Nurses, Bookkeepers, Admin Staff) | 🟢 **MINOR PRIZES ONLY** (Excluded from Grand Prize) | Per Teachers Committee policy update, non-teaching personnel are **included in all Minor Raffle draws** (household appliances, gift packs, cash incentives), but **strictly excluded from the Grand Prize**. |

> **Prize Categories:**  
> The system organizes all event prizes into two clear tiers:  
> 1. **★ Grand Prize:** The pinnacle jackpot awards of the celebration, reserved exclusively for Teaching Personnel on the main live stage.  
> 2. **● Minor Prizes:** General raffle items, household appliances, and sponsored goods, open to both Teaching and Non-Teaching Personnel across the live stage and the Pre-Draw station.

> **Can a teacher win twice?**  
> **NO.** Once a participant wins any prize, the system marks them as a winner and blocks their name from subsequent draws (unless the committee enables multiple wins in Admin Settings).

> **What happens if a prize is forfeited?**  
> If an absent or ineligible winner forfeits a prize at the claims desk or stage, clicking **"Mark as Forfeited"** automatically returns **+1 unit back to the available inventory**, allowing the prize to be redrawn immediately for another deserving participant.

## 🏢 The 4 Workstations Explained Simply

### 1. The Gate Attendance Desk (Gymnasium Entrances)
- **Who is there:** Gate Registration Marshals.
- **What they do:**
  1. As teachers arrive, the marshal scans the teacher's badge using a barcode gun or camera.
  2. If the badge is valid: A cheerful **chime sounds**, a green box appears, and the teacher is marked **PRESENT**.
  3. If someone tries to scan the same badge twice: A loud **warning buzz sounds**, saying *"Already Checked-in at Gate 1 at 7:15 AM!"* This prevents double-entries.
  4. If a teacher forgot their badge: The marshal simply types their surname or DepEd ID in the search box and clicks **"Check In"**.

### 2. The Stage & Big Screen (Gymnasium LED Wall)
- **Who is there:** Emcee, Committee Chairperson, and Projector Operator.
- **What happens on screen:**
  - The operator selects the prize (e.g. *₱10,000 Cash Prize* or *55-inch Smart TV*).
  - The emcee leads the countdown: **"3... 2... 1... SPIN!"**
  - Names spin rapidly across the district cards with audio ticking sounds.
  - The winning name snaps into bold letters, colorful confetti explodes across the screen, and victory fanfare plays!
  - For the ultimate Grand Prize, the screen transforms into a **Single Hero Card** filling the entire LED wall!

### 3. The Pre-Draw Room (Committee Secretariat Desk)
- **Why do we need this?**  
  If we have 200 electric fans, 100 rice cookers, and 300 grocery packs, drawing them one-by-one on stage would take 5 hours and bore the audience.
- **How it works:**
  - In the committee room with official audit witnesses, the secretariat draws Minor Prizes in rapid batches of 10 to 50 names.
  - Both Teaching and Non-Teaching personnel are eligible for these draws.
  - An official summary sheet is printed immediately and posted on the gym bulletin boards.
  - Winners are instantly sent to the Claims Desk so teachers can pick up their items quietly while the main program continues.

### 4. The Prize Claims Desk (Gymnasium Side Booth)
- **Who is there:** Disbursing Officers and Claims Marshals.
- **What they do:**
  1. The winning teacher walks to the claims desk.
  2. The officer looks up their name or scans their winning stub.
  3. The officer inspects their DepEd ID, PRC License, or Government ID.
  4. The teacher signs the official printed claim acknowledgment receipt.
  5. The physical prize or cash envelope is released!

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Committee Peace of Mind)

### Q1: What happens if the gymnasium Wi-Fi disconnects?
**Answer:** **Zero problem.** The system is designed to work completely offline. Every teacher's check-in, draw result, and claim record is saved directly inside the laptop. Even if the internet goes down for the entire day, the raffle will continue running without stopping. Once the internet returns, it updates the cloud database automatically.

### Q2: What happens if an absent teacher is drawn on stage?
**Answer:** The committee will follow its announced guidelines. If your rule is *"Must be present to win"*, the operator clicks the **"Redraw"** button. The system discards the absent name, leaves the database untouched, and immediately draws a new name.

### Q3: Can a teacher claim a prize on behalf of an absent colleague (Proxy Claim)?
**Answer:** Yes, if permitted by the committee. The Claims Desk has a dedicated **"Proxy Claim"** option. The officer enters the proxy's name, relationship (e.g. *Co-Teacher / School Head*), and notes that a signed authorization letter was presented. Both names are saved on the printed signed receipt for complete audit compliance.

### Q4: What if an unlisted teacher arrives at the gate?
**Answer:** If a newly hired or transfer teacher's name was missed during initial encoding, the gate marshal can click **"Add Teacher"**, type their DepEd ID and Name, and click Save. They are immediately registered, marked present, and eligible to win in less than 30 seconds.

### Q5: Can the IT technician or operator secretly manipulate who wins?
**Answer:** **Strictly impossible.** The system logs every single spin, draw timestamp, and confirmed winner into an immutable audit trail. At the end of the day, an official report is printed for the Commission on Audit (COA), the Municipal Mayor, and DepEd District Supervisors to review and sign.
